This policy explains what information {{ site_name }} (operated by {{ need(operator_name, "OPERATOR_NAME") }}) handles when you use it, why, and for how long. The service is designed to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.

What we process

  • The file you upload and its contents, so we can store it and let people with the link download it.
  • File details: the file name, size, the type reported by your browser, and the upload time. These are shown on download and used to apply the retention period.
  • A one-way hash of your delete key. The key itself is shown to you once and is not stored in readable form.
  • Your IP address and basic request data (such as time, URL and browser user agent). These are used to limit abuse through rate limiting and may appear in our hosting and storage providers' operational logs.

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details to upload a file.

Why we process it

  • To provide the service you requested: storing your file and delivering it to people with the link.
  • To keep the service secure and available, including preventing abuse and investigating misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as responding to valid legal requests.

  • Provider logs are kept according to those providers' own retention policies.

Who can see your file

{% if public_delivery %} Files are stored with Cloudflare R2 and delivered from a public storage domain under a long, random address. {% else %} Files are stored in a private storage bucket. Each download uses a short-lived signed link that is created when the download link is opened. {% endif %} Anyone who has the download link can download the file, so share it only with people you trust. We do not inspect, index or sell uploaded files, but we may remove a file if we are told it breaks the law or our Terms.

Service providers

  • Render hosts the web application.
  • Cloudflare R2 stores uploaded files.

These providers process data on our behalf, and data may be processed in countries other than your own.

Browser storage

If you switch between light and dark themes, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. See the Cookie Policy.

Your choices and rights

You can delete a file at any time with its delete key. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. To make a request or ask a question, contact {{ contact() }}. Because we don't collect accounts or contact details, we may need the download link to identify a file.
